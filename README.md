An open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built by an AI agent for
the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) gta6-01 bounty. Leo Cruz is
back in Solmar Bay with $40 in his pocket and a debt he cannot pay: steal
cars, outrun the Solmar Bay Police, and run four jobs for Mama Rosa to buy
his freedom.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. No build step, no network requests: every
script, texture and sound is a file under `game/` (three.js is vendored as
`game/vendor/three.min.js`).

## Play

- **WASD** move / drive · **Mouse** drag to look · **E** enter / steal a car
- **Click** to shoot · **Shift** to run · **P** pause · **M** mute
- **R** restart mission · **F1** save (progress also saves after every job)

The city has traffic and pedestrians, a wanted level with police who chase
and ram, four story missions, combat, a HUD with a minimap, and a save
system. Everything — the city of Solmar Bay, its people, its story — is
original work made for this entry.
